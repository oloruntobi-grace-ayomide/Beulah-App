Privacy & Cookie Notice

How we use cookies and similar technologies

We use necessary cookies and similar technologies to keep this website secure, remember basic preferences, and improve your experience.

Necessary website storage

Some cookies are needed for security, form protection, session handling, and basic website features. These help the site work correctly and are not used for advertising.

Basic preferences

We may remember simple choices in your browser, such as whether you have already seen a notice or counselling prompt. This helps avoid showing the same prompt repeatedly.

Third-party services

We may use trusted services such as Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection and payment providers such as Paystack or Stripe when donations are available. These services may process technical information needed to provide their service.

Your choice

You can clear cookies and site data from your browser settings at any time. If we later add analytics or marketing tracking, we should ask for a clearer opt-in choice before those non-essential tools are used.
